# SwasthyaSetu in 5 lines

1. Indian families keep health info scattered across paper prescriptions, lab PDFs and
   hospital queues - unreadable, untracked, and stressful in an emergency.
2. SwasthyaSetu is one bridge: it reads prescriptions and lab reports with AI in
   English and Hindi, builds a family health timeline, reminds medicines, and carries
   an offline emergency card.
3. It helps every Indian family - especially those managing elders' medicines and
   chronic conditions across members.
4. Real today: AI document reading (BharatCode Qwen vision), timeline, reminders,
   family profiles, emergency QR, safe symptom triage.
5. The hackathon build proves the reader and the family model - and honestly demos the
   appointment, queue and hospital screens it is designed to connect.

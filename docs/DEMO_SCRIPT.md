# SwasthyaSetu - 3-minute demo script

1. **Open on the Scan tab.** "Every Indian family has a drawer full of prescriptions and
   lab reports nobody can read a month later. SwasthyaSetu is one bridge for all of it."
2. **Upload the sample prescription** (or any real prescription photo) and click
   "Read with AI". The BharatCode Qwen vision model reads it live.
   - Wow moment: the read comes back in plain English AND Hindi, with salt names,
     cheaper Jan Aushadhi generics, and a caught paracetamol-overlap caution.
   - If the shared model is queued, say so honestly and use `?autodemo=1` - the same
     output from a precomputed read, labeled demo mode on screen.
3. **Timeline tab.** "Every document the family scans becomes a health timeline; lab
   values plot as trends, so a rising TSH is visible at a glance."
4. **Reminders + Family.** "Medicine reminders per family member - dad's BP tablet and
   my vitamins live in the same app, stored on the device."
5. **Emergency card.** Show the QR: "Works offline. Anyone helping in an emergency
   scans it and sees blood group, allergies, medicines, emergency contact."
6. **Symptom check.** Type "chest pain" - show it refuses to diagnose and says call
   108/112. Then "fever and cold" - suggests a general physician, triage only.
7. **Appointments + Queue.** Book a demo slot, show the token: "4 patients ahead, ~32
   min - the app pings you when 2 are left." Say clearly: this is simulated to show
   the flow hospitals could plug into.
8. **Hospital dashboard.** "And this is where a hospital's own screen plugs in - beds,
   vitals, billing." Point at the DEMO banner: all simulated.
9. Close: "The AI reading, timeline, reminders, family profiles and emergency card are
   real and running today. Booking, queue and hospital screens are honest demos of the
   bridge we want hospitals to join."

Upload a prescription or lab report

Take a photo of a doctor's prescription or a lab report. SwasthyaSetu explains it in plain English and Hindi, lists every medicine with its salt name, suggests cheaper Jan Aushadhi generics, and flags lab values outside range.

Safety: SwasthyaSetu is an understanding aid, not medical advice. AI explanations and the symptom check can be wrong - always confirm medicines, doses, reports and symptoms with a qualified doctor or pharmacist. Appointments, queue, lab prices and hospital screens use simulated demo data. Documents stay on your device (browser local storage); they are sent only to the BharatCode model API for reading.

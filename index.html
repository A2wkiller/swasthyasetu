Upload a prescription or lab report

Take a photo of a doctor's prescription or a lab report. SwasthyaSetu explains it in plain English and Hindi, lists every medicine with its salt name, suggests cheaper Jan Aushadhi generics, and flags lab values outside range.

Health timeline

Every document read is saved on this device per family member. Lab values like TSH or fasting sugar are plotted so trends are visible at a glance.

Lab trends

Medicine reminders

Medicines from a scanned prescription can become reminders in one tap, or add your own. While this page is open, due reminders alert here.

Family profiles

Keep everyone's documents, reminders and emergency cards separate. The active profile is used across the app.

Emergency card

One card with what a doctor or helper needs first: blood group, conditions, allergies, current medicines and an emergency contact. Show the QR - it encodes the same text, works offline.

Symptom pre-check

Triage only - this does NOT diagnose. It tells you how urgently to see a doctor and which specialist fits.
